## Why Flat-Pack Collapsible Magnetic Boxes Cut Freight CBM by ~75%

Using `CartonCBM`, 500 assembled book-style rigid gift boxes (`260 × 200 × 90 mm`) require roughly **3.1–3.4 CBM** of export carton volume, whereas flat-pack foldable magnetic rigid boxes ([Luxury Magnetic Closure Boxes](https://www.bestpackfactory.com/products/luxury-magnetic-boxes.html)) compress into **~0.80–0.90 CBM**—dramatically lowering Sea DDP and Air DDP landed unit cost.
